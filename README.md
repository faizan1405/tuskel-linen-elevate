# Tuskel Redesign Project

Create a complete, premium, clean, modern, multi-page e-commerce website redesign for:

Brand: TUSKEL

Existing website: https://tuskel.com/

Business: Premium men’s pure linen and linen-blend shirts

Market: India

Brand positioning: Effortless sophistication, breathable comfort and timeless menswear.

Main Objective

Redesign Tuskel into a polished premium D2C fashion brand website that feels editorial, sophisticated and trustworthy.

This must not look like a generic WooCommerce template, ordinary clothing store, SaaS website or AI-generated landing page.

The experience should feel comparable to a modern luxury fashion label while remaining easy to navigate, fast and conversion-focused.

Create a complete working sample with dedicated pages, functional navigation, interactions, product filtering, cart experience and responsive layouts.

Core Brand Feeling

Tuskel should communicate:

* Quiet luxury

* Premium summer fashion

* Breathable comfort

* Modern Indian menswear

* Refined simplicity

* Natural fabrics

* Effortless formal and smart-casual dressing

Use the central brand idea:

“Made for warmer days. Designed for sharper ones.”

Supporting line:

“Premium linen shirts crafted for effortless comfort, refined style and modern living.”

Do not use excessive marketing language or generic filler copy.

Visual Direction

Use an elegant editorial fashion layout with:

* Warm ivory and linen-inspired backgrounds

* Charcoal or near-black typography

* Soft beige, sand and stone shades

* Muted olive or warm brown as a restrained accent

* Product colours used naturally as secondary accents

* Large high-quality fashion photography

* Fabric close-ups and texture shots

* Generous white space

* Strong typography hierarchy

* Clean grid-based layouts

* Thin borders and subtle dividers

* Minimal shadows

* Sophisticated asymmetrical compositions where appropriate

Avoid:

* Bright gradients

* Neon colours

* Excessive glassmorphism

* Too many rounded cards

* Oversized headings that occupy the whole screen

* Crowded product sections

* Heavy animations

* Generic stock-template layouts

* Unnecessary badges and icons

Typography

Use a refined serif font for editorial headings and a clean sans-serif font for body copy and UI.

Suggested pairing:

* Headings: Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display

* Body and interface: Manrope, Inter or DM Sans

Headings should feel elegant but remain readable.

Photography Direction

Use premium editorial images featuring men wearing linen shirts in:

* Modern architectural spaces

* Naturally lit interiors

* Refined outdoor summer environments

* Minimal office or smart-casual settings

* Close-up fabric and stitching details

Images should feel natural, aspirational and fashion-focused.

Avoid overly posed, obviously artificial or visually inconsistent images.

Use consistent image treatment and product photography ratios throughout the website.

Animation Direction

Use Framer Motion for refined interactions.

Required animations:

* Soft hero image zoom

* Staggered hero text reveal

* Clip-path image reveals

* Scroll-triggered section entrances

* Gentle parallax on campaign imagery

* Smooth product image hover transition

* Button and link micro-interactions

* Animated cart drawer

* Smooth accordion transitions

* Refined page transitions

* Sticky product information on desktop

* Sticky Add to Cart bar on mobile

* Subtle number and price transitions

* Animated mega menu

* Smooth mobile navigation drawer

Animations should generally last between 300 and 700 milliseconds.

Do not animate every element.

Avoid:

* Constant floating objects

* Excessive cursor effects

* Aggressive horizontal scrolling

* Heavy 3D scenes

* Long loading intros

* Animations that delay shopping

* Motion that causes layout shift

Respect the user’s reduced-motion preference.
